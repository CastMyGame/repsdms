## Current limits and sequence

The currency bridge is the first focused Phase 4B-2 implementation. Gmail grant ownership, notification actor provenance, canonical recipient projection and worker-to-provider APIs are not implemented by that bridge. Root tenant metadata alone does not make dispatch safe. Finish these as coordinated email/notification work, then integrate case/assignment creation, closure and background routes against the reviewed mapping coverage. Complete Stripe compatibility independently without interpreting payment as school authorization. Only after all dependent routes/workers, reviewed pilot mapping and controller-level tests pass may the coordinated ID-only cutover replace the current guard.

Do not use an email address, school display name, existing spotter status, Stripe metadata or payment success as ownership evidence. Preserve encrypted authorizations, receipts, embedded histories and billing IDs; unresolved associations remain blocked. No backfill or mixed legacy/canonical writer rollout is authorized by these preparation documents.
